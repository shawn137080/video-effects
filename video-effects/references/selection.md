# Choose an effect for the content

Ask what must become clearer in this beat. Use the source's composition and
actual action; matching a keyword to a template is insufficient.

| Content job | Candidates | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|
| One emphatic spoken point | Snap Zoom, Headline Storm | Text competes with speech, or every word already has impact |
| Explain a sequence | Checklist | Long items cannot be read in the available hold |
| Show evidence while speaking | B-roll, Phone, Green Screen, Media Wall | No real supporting media exists |
| Explain numbers | Growth Metric | Numbers/provenance are missing or misleading |
| Point to a specific detail | Spotlight, Freeze Callout, Doodle | Pointer cannot be aligned to a visible target |
| Demonstrate time | Timer | The displayed duration is fabricated or obscures the hand |
| Show a before/after | Before / After | Inputs do not represent a valid comparison |
| Reveal a new setting | Studio Reveal, Portal, Through Cut | There is no meaningful incoming setting/shot |
| Add depth or playful identity | Orbit, Frame Escape, Paper Cutout, Text Behind | Matte edges or card sources are poor |
| Stylized presentation | Magazine, Liquid Glass, Dolly Zoom, Frame Break | Decoration replaces the content or hides the face |

Recommend one primary effect, explain its timing and assets, and include no
effect as an option when the source already carries the message. Glass belongs
on useful panels; do not turn every mechanism into glass. Keep natural portrait
color unless the user requests a grade. Preserve whole words and enough time to
read. Source actions should determine onset, peak and return; whole-scene motion
and child-object motion are different layers.

Title hierarchy: a short main phrase, optional smaller support, then the visual
proof. Do not duplicate three competing labels for the same effect. Effects
that intentionally take over the screen (Chat, Portal) can cover the presenter;
other overlays should be checked against eyes, mouth and hands.
